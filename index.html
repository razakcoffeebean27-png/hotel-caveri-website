About Hotel Caveri Inn

A Comfortable Stay in the Heart of Madikeri

Hotel Caveri Inn is located opposite Rajaseat Garden, Stuart Hill, Madikeri, Karnataka.

The hotel offers a range of accommodation options suitable for individual travellers, couples, families and groups visiting Coorg.

With convenient access to nearby restaurants and cafés, Hotel Caveri Inn provides a practical base for exploring Madikeri and the surrounding attractions of Coorg.

Guests can also make use of the hotel's travel desk, Wi-Fi, car parking, express check-in, 24-hour reception and room service.
